Hex Dominion 2 — Art Gallery

Dev gallery for game/render/{palette,tiles,sprites,clouds}.js — a hand-painted tabletop diorama, drawn procedurally with no image assets (DESIGN §7). This page renders a mock island (generated here only, never written into game/world) to exercise the whole stack: elevation skirts, rivers, roads, coast foam, decorations, territory, settlements, banners, squads and clouds.
