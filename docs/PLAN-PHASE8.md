# Phase 8: Fast, clear, and a bit more to find (design + contract)

Status: **planned 2026-10-04**, to start once Phase 7 is deployed. Lead: the Claude session. Engineers: **performance + codex** (integration) and **content** (sim/meta).

Pace (from the user): build until playable, one QA pass at the end (the full deploy gate), then the user playtests.

**Why this phase.** Seven phases added a lot of systems:
- **Load:** the game now loads about 258 files with no build step, and the per-tick meta work grew with every phase.
- **Clarity:** each system is taught by a one-shot tutorial hint, so a player who missed one, or comes back after a week, has nowhere to look things up.
- **Variety:** the Boon pool is small enough that a dynasty sees most of it.

Phase 8 makes the game **fast** on a mid-range phone, **clear** with an in-game Codex, and **fresher** with more Boons, Relics and events.

---

## 8A. Performance (measure first, then fix the worst)

**Budgets:**
- **First load**, with Chrome's throttling (Fast 4G, 4× CPU slowdown), on a cold cache:
  - the title screen interactive in under **3.5 s**
  - the map playable in under **6 s**
- **Frame time** on the map and in a big battle (about 14 squads, an Ashen fight with wisps, a Dragon fight), at 4× CPU slowdown: a median frame under **20 ms** and a 95th percentile under **33 ms**.
- **Meta tick:** under **2 ms** per frame on average in a late D2 state with every system active.
- **Save size:** under **200 KB** in a late D3 state; report the current size.

**Method:**
- A `tools/perf.mjs` (CDP, no dependencies) that measures all of the above reproducibly and prints a table.
- Run it before changing anything, fix the worst offenders, then run it again.

**Allowed fixes:**
- `<link rel="modulepreload">` for the boot-critical graph, generated by a small script (still no build step at runtime)
- lazy `import()` of panels and dialogs not needed at boot (ceremony, Codex, Reliquary, draft)
- caching of per-frame computations, and fewer canvas state changes
- throttling the meta tick
- trimming saved state

**Not allowed:** a bundler, dependencies, or changes to the sim's determinism.

## 8B. The Codex (look anything up)

**Where:** a **Codex** entry in Settings, plus a "?" link from every panel header that opens the Codex at its topic.

**Topics:** one short page per system, each with an icon, 2–4 plain sentences, the key numbers (generated from config) and a small illustration if one exists:
- **Battles:** sending, Rally, powers, crowns
- **Your realm:** income, the War Council, prosperity and Festivals, Works
- **Defense:** raids, defense battles, occupation, fortifications, militia, the Steward
- **People and rivals:** Generals and abilities, Renown, rival leaders, Grudges and Vendettas
- **The map:** region types, battle twists, world events
- **Goals:** the Bounty Board, the Conquest Streak, Deeds
- **Dynasties:** founding, Edicts, Legacy, Challenges, Quick Conquest
- **Boons and Relics:** Boons, Duos, Relics, the Reliquary
- **The Ashen Host**

**Discovery:** topics stay locked ("Not yet discovered") until the player has met the system, so spoilers stay hidden. A "Seen it before? Revisit the tutorial hints" toggle resets one-shot hints.

**Constraints:** keyboard and screen-reader friendly, and it fits a 360 px phone.

## 8C. More to find (content top-up)

- **+8 Boons**, bringing the pool to 32, each changing behaviour. Ideas, which sim/meta may replace with better ones:
  - **Vanguard:** the first squad of each battle is +50%.
  - **Supply Wagons:** supply lines send 25% more.
  - **Rearguard:** retreating keeps 50% of the troops in the field.
  - **Tower Sappers:** enemy towers lose 30% range while you hold an adjacent site.
  - **War Drums:** every power you cast gives +10% speed for 5 s.
  - **Spoils of War:** each crown earned gives +1 Renown.
  - **Last Stand:** your keep (in defenses) gets +40% defence below 25% troops.
  - **Cartographer:** Quick Conquest also works on Fair regions.
- **+2 Duos** built from the new Boons.
- **+4 Relics**, bringing the pool to 12, so the Reliquary takes several dynasties to fill:
  - Merchant's Scale: Merchant deals 30% cheaper.
  - Warden's Bell: defenses' siege timer −20%.
  - Twin Crowns: Swift's par +20 s.
  - Seal of the Margrave: Ashen continents only; the Barrow Keep's Rising every 30 s instead of 20 s.
- **+2 world events:**
  - **Deserters:** a rival's troops defect; their next raid is 30% smaller, or you gain a free Muster everywhere.
  - **Harvest Festival:** for 5 minutes, prosperity grows twice as fast; opt-in, and it costs gold.

  Each with leader lines and Chronicle entries.
- **Pacing guard:** D1–D3 unchanged within the existing targets (D1 1.1–1.6 h, waits over 40 min on at most 2 of 12 seeds), and still about 10–14 Boons owned at the end of D1.

## Contract

**content (sim/meta):** new Boons, Duos, Relics and events in config plus pure modules, wired through `boonMods` and `events.js` as before, with tests. Update `docs/briefs/phase7-hookup.md` (or write `phase8-hookup.md`) with new event kinds and any new `boonTriggered` ids.

**perf + codex (integration):**
- `tools/perf.mjs`, the measured before/after table, and the fixes (8A)
- the Codex (8B), with text built from config numbers wherever a number appears
- icons and visuals for the 8C content once its config lands
- tutorial: one hint pointing at the Codex after the first battle

**QA:** the final pass is exactly the deploy gate (`npm test`, the full `node tools/check.mjs`, `node tools/check.mjs --base=temp`), plus a new `--only=codex` section and the perf table. Gallery: `screenshots/phase8/`.
